About Me

Education:
BASc from Quest University Canada 2016
PhD from the University of Denver 2025

Background:
I am a curious and dedicated scientist interested in applying my expertise in data analytics to address complex problems impacting society today.

With the belief that integrating diverse viewpoints leads to stronger, more effective solutions I chose to earn a Bachelor of Liberal Arts and Sciences. In this four-year degree I not only built a strong multidisciplinary foundation - enabling me to communicate effectively across disciplines - I also designed my own course plan focused on developing my critical thinking, coding and problem-solving skills.

As a problem solver fascinated by complex systems, I knew I ultimately wanted to pursue a career in which I could utilize data science and my multidisciplinary background to tackle big problems. However, I didn't just want to help, I wanted the expertise to tease apart the intricacies of an issue and the skills to pioneer approaches for meaningful results.

Guided by a belief that exploration and innovation are central to what makes us human, I elected to assemble my desired skillset by earning a PhD in the challenging, but dazzling, field of Astrophysics. Throughout this rigorous process I found research well equipped me with firsthand experience working through the entire life-cycle of multifaceted projects while strengthening my ability to communicate across all levels of expertise.

Now I am transitioning to the industry workforce as an adept computational/data scientist proficient in conducting independent research, adapting when challenges arise, and remaining productive despite uncertain or evolving environments.

More information can be found throughout my website.
Please reach out if you wish to learn more.
